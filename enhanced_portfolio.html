Things you should know

Time

Expect projects to be completed within 1-10 days of requesting. I'm avaliable Mondays - Fridays [11 AM - 11 PM].

Revisions

2 free revisions included, however you will be constantly sent progress updates to ensure you're getting what you want!

Delivery

Project file [RBXM], Optional Video Showcase with/without watermark [MOV and MP4 formats avaliable].

Prices

Basic

$5-$20 USD or 1,500-6,000 robux | Simpler effects and particles. Usually these don't take more than a day to produce [E.g. Fireball, Lighting Strike, Beam, Slash, etc.].

Advanced

$20-$60 USD or 6,000-18,000 robux | These projects often take more time, though still have fast production time. Includes medium sized auras and typical polished creations. Can also be a combination of several "Basic" creations.

Complex

$60+ or 18,000+ robux | Projects in this category take minimum several days of production. Usually they're also much bigger and sophisticated in terms of effects. Includes FREE modelling!
